WELCOME TO PONG-EXTREME-v1
In this game you are in control of the left white paddle and you are trying to score 20 points to win agains the CPU.
By pressing the button 'W' you go up and by pressing 'S' you go down.
If you are compiling in Code::Blocks or another IDE, copy the .dll files from this folder to the same location where your final .exe is generated.
